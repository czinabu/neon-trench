# Roadmap

Where Neon Trench goes next. Kept short on purpose: only the near term gets detail. The title screen's
**Roadmap** tab (`js/data/roadmap.js`) shows the same list; keep the two in step.

## Now

Depth and feel before more systems.

- **Campaign tuning.** One full run from the first sortie to the Kiru Gate: fix pacing, difficulty spikes and anything that breaks. First pass in: Reactor Run opens with a gentler warmup (single beams and pillars, more room; `warmup` in `js/data/missions.js`), a crossbeam on your line calls CLIMB, DIVE, LEFT or RIGHT on Easy and Normal (`BEAM_WARN`), fleet battles keep enemy hunters coming at you once the op's fighters are down (Sovereign sends its squadron leader instead; they used to be gone 20 seconds into Broadside; `FLEET_HUNT`), and a lance charging on you names the ship on the warning line and hits softer on Easy and Normal (`LANCE_DMG`, all in `js/config.js`). Includes the open tuning passes: trench routes (`FORK_*`, `PAD_*`), `DIFFICULTY`, where promotions fall, `SECTOR_CARRY` repairs and the fleet prices in `js/data/dock.js`. The playtest bot (`npm run playtest`) now gives bridge orders, dodges and clears every fleet op, so it can check pacing across the whole campaign; next it needs to learn the long capital ops (Ace Duel, Shipbreaker, Dark Approach), which still time out.
- **Act V and the asteroid field.** The campaign goes on past Sovereign: Act V, The Drift, is five ops into the Kiru Drift after the Directorate, the board that owned the syndicate (Into the Drift, Claim Jumpers, Foundry Run, Rockfall, The Directorate), on a second page of the sector map with its own side targets, two new cutscenes and crew lines. Most of it is flown in a new battlefield: an asteroid field of 170 solid rocks (`js/field.js`, `FIELD` in `js/config.js`). Ore rocks with amber seams blow when shot (three hits or a missile), hurting every fighter in reach and setting off ore nearby, and big rocks and ore stop capital broadsides, so fleets fight for clear lines. Foundry Run is a new trench, the Directorate's foundry, hot with pistons, wall guns and bombers. Contracts can now be dealt in the rock, and Skirmish has a Battlefield option. Next: playtest Act V's pacing, the rock density and the ore blast damage.
- **Game feel.** Stronger hit feedback, camera shake, and sound and music that follow the situation. In: a white-hot pop on every enemy hit that doesn't kill, and the danger score when a lance is charging on you. Next: a cockpit framing pass (the dash fills about 40% of the view), now that a phone's attitude ball sits low on the dash instead of over the gunsight.
- **Upgrade shop and unlocks.** One upgrade shop: the hangar's Upgrades tab sells ship upgrades, wing and HALCYON upgrades and, once the sector map opens, each destroyer's refits, in one scrolling list you can drive with a pad (`js/shop.js`). The Fleet station keeps repairs, pilots and recruits, with a button into the shop. Hull paints are on the Paint tab (buy four with credits, earn two by clearing Ace Duel and Sovereign; `js/data/paints.js`). A fourth hull is in: STILETTO, a dogfighter with forward-swept wings, a V-tail, its own cockpit and the tightest turn in the fleet, unlocked for 7 parts once Ace Duel is cleared (`js/data/ships.js`). Hulls you haven't unlocked stay a surprise in the hangar: no model, stats or description until you own one, and STILETTO's row can't be picked until Ace Duel is cleared. Wingman and HALCYON upgrades only show in the shop once the campaign brings them in (Shipbreaker for the wing, Hold Halcyon for HALCYON). Next: playtest the shop prices and the STILETTO.
- **A living HALCYON.** The walkable deck now follows the campaign. The hull HALCYON carries between fleet battles shows aboard (scorch, dead and flickering lamps, sparking conduits, welders, smoke, a patched bridge window and, at its worst, emergency light), repairs at the Fleet station clear it and Chief Oda says so, and the deck goes to battle stations when the next op puts HALCYON in the fight. The destroyers out the windows are the ones in your fleet, named with their hull and burning when hurt, and their captains report as holograms round the bridge's holo table. A new ready room off the spine has the next op's briefing screen, the Contracts board (once Acts I to III are clear), a side bets board and the reserve pilots DRIFT and CINDER. Crew offer a side bet on the next op (no boost, a x6 combo, hull left, HALCYON's hull, kills over the count): take it by talking to them again, and it shows in the briefing and pays at the debrief; losing costs nothing (`js/deckwar.js`; `DECK_WAR`, `DECK_STATUS` and `DECK_BETS` in `js/data/deck.js`). Next: walk out to the catapult to launch and climb out after landing with the deck crew reacting to how it went, a trophy wall in the spine that fills as you clear ops, and walking the deck by touch.

## Next

- **New-pilot onboarding.** A new pilot now opens on The story so far, a one-minute mini-movie of how the Rim fell (the Rim's carriers keeping the lanes ten years ago, the Helios-Kiru syndicate dropping in behind THRONE, the carrier CONCORD breaking up, Kestrel's gold flight hunting the last Rim pilots, and HALCYON jumping out with an empty deck to sit dark for six weeks), in five chapters, skippable on its own and always in the Campaign log. It ends on the prologue's own opening shot, and the prologue cutscene follows (HALCYON dark over the planet, your fighter coming aboard, the neural link set up for later), the welcome card and the flight school offer, then a guided tour over the hub that clears as you do each step: look around, walk, talk to someone or use a station, open the datapad and open your first op (on the command center: pick a station, then Brief and launch), and what the briefing shows. Launch hands over to first-flight training in the cockpit, now worded for keyboard, gamepad or touch. Every part can be skipped, and Settings > Replay intro plays it all again, the movie included (`js/onboard.js`). Next: watch a first-time player go through it and trim what they skip.
- **Story moments.** Crew aboard the walkable HALCYON now have something new to say after key ops (18 story beats from Pike, Lark, Vale, Reyes, Chief Oda and Hale, said once each, data in `DECK_BEATS`, `js/data/deck.js`). Seven short cutscenes now play once at the turning points: HALCYON lighting up for your first launch, Kestrel's escape after Ace Duel, THRONE breaking apart, the fleet dropping in at the Kiru Gate, SOVEREIGN's end, the fleet easing into the Kiru Drift and ECLIPSE breaking up in the rock. They are letterboxed with crew subtitles, skippable, and can be watched again from the Campaign log (`js/data/cutscenes.js`). Every trench run now opens with a five-second flyby of what it strikes (the Helios-Kiru core, the north stack on Silent Run, a fighter base's launch tower, a contract's power station, the Drift foundry, THRONE's burning breach) that cross-fades on the same camera view into a dive into the trench mouth in the game itself. Each trench has its own slab round it (towers, a sensor sink, landing pads or a power plant), colours, crew callout and mix of hazards (Silent Run has more wall guns and fewer pistons, a fighter base more bombers and fighter waves, a power station more pistons and roll gates). The flyby skips with a key, a click or tap, or a pad button (Settings: Trench intros; `TRENCH_INTROS` in `js/data/cutscenes.js`). Next: more Kestrel encounters in the earlier acts. Pilot view is a neural link: the commander stays at HALCYON's holo table (the same table as on the walkable bridge) and flies a drone fighter, with a link-sync flash when you swap and a link-strength readout in the fight. Ideas: a jamming enemy that cuts the link, and a late mission flown for real with no link.
- **Replayability.** Survival is in Free Play: endless arena waves, ten kills a wave, tougher and more crowded each wave with aces from wave four, and your best wave is the record (`SURVIVAL` in `js/config.js`). Contracts are in Free Play too: a board of three procedural ops dealt from a six-letter code (sweeps, holds, capital strikes, ace hunts, carrier defence, trench and interior runs, fleet actions), each with a threat level, two bonuses and a payout in credits and parts; a repeat pays half, and typing a code flies someone else's board (`CONTRACTS` in `js/data/contracts.js`); about a third of the open-space contracts are flown in the asteroid field. Next: playtest contract pay and threat, and new objective types (escort, interceptor, time trial).
- **Gamepad and performance.** Gamepad flight is in: sticks, triggers and buttons for steering, throttle, fire, boost, flares, missiles, roll, view and pause, with a rumble on hits (`js/gamepad.js`, listed on the Controls screen). Pad support now covers the menus, the pause screen, Settings, the command bridge and walking HALCYON's deck (`js/padui.js`). Settings has gamepad sensitivity, invert Y and rumble, and key rebinding for flight keys, including mouse buttons and the wheel (`js/keybinds.js`), on a two-column Settings screen; the Controls screen shows your rebound keys. The performance pass has started: trench and interior segments are merged into a few meshes each, which cuts the trench from about 700 draw calls a frame to about 200, and phones and tablets now start at medium graphics. Medium and Low switch most effect lights and all but one engine light off (phones went from 13 lit point lights to 7), and restarting an op now frees the last run's meshes instead of leaking about 120 a restart in fleet battles. Next: more performance work, and a real-controller playtest of the new menu, deck and bridge controls.
- **Phone touch controls.** First pass is in: a floating steer stick on the left half of the screen, a THR strip for throttle (cruise holds the speed, Brake stops), roll buttons above Brake, Fire, Boost, Torp, Msl, Flare and Bridge buttons on the right, and Pause, Tgt and View at the top, with a trimmed HUD on touch screens (the objective box and progress rail no longer overlap the boost ladder, and the flight buttons only show in flight, not through the menus) (`js/input.js`, listed on the Controls screen). Phones and tablets are now detected more reliably (iPhone, iPad asking for the desktop site, Android), and the first touch turns the controls on if the check still misses; `?touch=1` or `?touch=0` forces it. Menus and the datapad were already tap-friendly. Next: playtest on a real phone, strafe, a portrait layout, walking HALCYON's deck by touch (the hub stays the command center on touch screens for now) and the command bridge by touch.
- **Gameplay tests.** Extend `npm test` with win and lose checks for each mission.
