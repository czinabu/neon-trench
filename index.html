Roll gates Match the slot's angle as you pass through for Overdrive: shields, a missile and faster guns.
Combos Chain kills quickly to raise the score multiplier.
Targeting Press R to select the hostile nearest your crosshair, and again to cycle. The target box shows its range, closing speed and hull, the lead ring follows it, and missiles lock only it.
Locking Middle click switches to missiles. Target an enemy with R, hold it in your sights until it locks, then left click. At the reactor, lock on and press F for the torpedo.
Incoming Hunters and aces fire homing missiles. Pop flares (G) to decoy them, break hard, boost away, or shoot them down. Flares recharge over time.
Pickups Wrecks drop shield, missile and score pickups. Fly through them to collect.
